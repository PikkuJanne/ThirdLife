# Controlled pilot metrics and evidence

Pilot telemetry is off. The current app saves its job decisions, typed
results and verification locally. The sanitized support archive is a
separately previewed per-case support copy. An optional aggregate pilot
metrics export remains pending.

Use case evidence within workshop custody to record whether required
stages completed, which capability remained pending and which outcome
was independently verified. Record exact source and artifact identity
alongside technical qualification results. Keep native results,
controlled fixture results and unavailable scenarios attributable.

Any future pilot metrics file requires an explicit preview of the exact
aggregate or pseudonymized fields. Confirm the recipient and transfer
scope before sharing. Raw jobs, workshop reports, device identifiers,
paths and recovery material stay in their current custody.
