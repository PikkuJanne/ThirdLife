## Completion scope

The test plan maps each broker-relevant abuse case to focused evidence or an
explicit limit. Session binding and cleanup cover the approved no-op scope.
Separate-process cross-account claims would require a verified distinct process
in the admitted same-machine guest. Filesystem races, future mutating actions,
broader workloads and administrator or operating-system compromise retain
their documented limits. Evidence retains fixed diagnostics and bounded
results. [Residual-risk register](../../../docs/security/threat-model.md#residual-risk-register)

TL-0313 is the next implementation action on the broker path. It connects the
approved plan, durable journal, consent, broker result and recovery. Ordinary
broker-dependent dispatch requires that integration, action-specific authority
and Windows consent, and the candidate-scoped TL-0314 privilege decision.
